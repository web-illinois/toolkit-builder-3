## Applications

The following sites were developed through a collaboration among the <a href="https://wigg.illinois.edu/">Web Implementation Guidelines Group (WIGG)</a> <span role="presentation">* </span>, <a href="https://www.techservices.illinois.edu/">Technology Services</a> <span role="presentation">* </span>, and <a href="https://itpartners.illinois.edu/">IT Partners</a> <span role="presentation">* </span>. Together, they provide shared tools for managing campus information and identifying website technologies.

* <a href="https://go.illinois.edu/resource-toolkit">Resource site </a> <span role="presentation">*</span> organizes and manages resources, publications, notes, frequently asked questions, people, and events.
* <a href="https://go.illinois.edu/course-toolkit">Course site </a> <span role="presentation">*</span> manages academic programs, credentials, courses, and curriculum requirements.
* <a href="https://go.illinois.edu/directory-toolkit">Directory site </a> <span role="presentation">*</span> manages employee profiles, office details, and organizational information for campus directories.
* <a href="https://go.illinois.edu/sitescanner-toolkit">Site Scanner </a> <span role="presentation">*</span> checks websites for hosting information, content management systems, Illinois Toolkit usage, and other technical characteristics.
